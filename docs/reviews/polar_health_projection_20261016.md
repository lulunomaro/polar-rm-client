## Concrete duplication and boundary

`BackendManager._publish_polar_authority` and `BackendManager.stats` independently
assembled the same diagnostic prefix: transport statistics, bridge freshness,
formatted/sorted segment counters, dropped segments and six decoder counters.
The new `capture_polar_link_health` owns that shared projection. Its two typed
suppliers expose only transport/bridge diagnostic sources; it receives no manager,
RobotState, registry, native handle, control policy or teardown object.

Suppliers are called at each original observation point. Capturing either source
eagerly, or copying all counters into an early snapshot, would alter failures and
potentially report stale values. The helper preserves ordered repeated reads,
source-mapping expansion, boolean conversion, sorted hexadecimal segment labels,
integer counter conversion and collision overrides. It is a fresh ordered
observation, not a claim of an atomic cross-thread snapshot.

The two callers intentionally keep different tails. Authority is captured before
state writes by the tick path but after decoder counters by runtime stats. Motion
is tick-only. Timestamp-roll observation remains after those caller-owned fields.
The earlier live-native-video update in stats and all safety/state effects stay
in the manager. No authorization, transport lifetime, protocol or wire behavior
was moved or changed.

## Characterization before replacement

The exact two original methods are retained in
`tests/fixtures/polar_health_baseline.txt`, with an immutable digest checked by
`tests/test_polar_health_characterization.py`. Two methods passed before the
production change. Their differential observations cover both normal and estopped
outputs, and all **192** read/call/conversion/write failure prefixes (141 in stats,
51 in tick publication), comparing original exception identity, effect ordering
and partial state. Source methods and the fixture are not regenerated by tests.

Four detached-owner tests additionally cover collision overrides, output key
format/order, no copying of unrelated nested transport values, repeated dynamic
source suppliers, successive fresh captures, malformed count failures, and a
fresh-process import check excluding transport/protocol/model/GUI dependencies.
